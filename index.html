About

Hi, I'm Gordon.

I'm pursuing a BSc in Physics and a BBA in Information Systems and Analytics at the University of Hong Kong, with a particular interest in using technology to solve business problems.

My experience spans both sides of that intersection. On the technical side, I wrote C/C++ firmware for the HKU RoboMaster Team's robotic dart launcher and explored computer science and technical entrepreneurship at Stanford Summer Session. On the commercial side, I lead HKU's University Business Consulting Club and co-founded Alpha Flow, an early-stage project in AI-driven quantitative research.

I'm currently seeking internships in technology consulting, digital transformation, and strategy & operations in Hong Kong — especially roles that bridge technical teams and business decision-makers.
